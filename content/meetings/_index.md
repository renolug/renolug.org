subtitle: "When and where we get together."

---

RLUG meetings are free, open to everyone, and informal. Expect a mix of short
talks and demos, open Q&A and troubleshooting, and plenty of time to just talk
shop. You do not need to be an expert — bring your questions, your projects, or
just your curiosity.

Got an idea for a talk, a demo, or a topic you'd like to see covered? We'd love
to hear it — reach out on the
[mailing list](https://groups.google.com/g/renolug) or in the
[Signal group](/signal).
